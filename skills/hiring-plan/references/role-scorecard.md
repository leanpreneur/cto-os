## Checks before you post

- [ ] Two people who never discussed the role would score each competency the same way.
- [ ] Every must-have competency is tested in at least two interviews, by two different people.
- [ ] The level matches the ladder, and the outcomes are right for that level.
- [ ] The pay band is approved.
- [ ] The AI policy for the loop is written.
- [ ] The interviewers are named and trained.
