# Principles

Ten rules that every artifact in this repository follows.

1. **Diagnose before prescribing.** Spend the first weeks learning how the system and the people actually work. Change what you have measured.

2. **Write decisions down.** A decision that lives in someone's head gets relitigated. A short written record with context, options and consequences ends the argument and trains the next hire.

3. **Move fast on reversible choices, slowly on irreversible ones.** Pick a library in an afternoon. Pick a data model, a cloud commitment or a reorganization with care.

4. **Measure flow, not activity.** Track how quickly a change reaches customers and how often it breaks. Lines of code, tickets closed and hours logged describe effort, not results.

5. **One owner per system.** Every service, pipeline and process has a named owner and a named backup. Shared ownership means no ownership.

6. **Treat technical debt as a budget line.** Name it, size it, fund a fixed share of capacity for it each quarter, and report it to the CEO like any other liability.

7. **Make the organization explain itself.** Runbooks, architecture diagrams, onboarding plans and decision records let the team run when you are not in the room.

8. **State what you will not do.** A plan with no exclusions is a wish list. Publish the cut line with the priorities.

9. **Report outcomes to the CEO and the board.** Customers, revenue, reliability, cost, risk and people. Translate technical work into those terms every time.

10. **Use evidence over opinion, including your own.** Label each artifact Draft, Used or Proven. Revise it when reality disagrees.
