# Example: a promotion packet that ends in "not yet"

**Made by:** written by hand, to show a filled [promotion packet](../../templates/promotion-packet.md) and the conversation that follows. P-17 and ExampleCo are invented. Dates and results are made up.

## Promotion Packet: P-17, E2 to E3

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Manager | M-04 |
| Sponsor | A director outside the team |
| Review period | 2026-01-01 to 2026-09-30 |
| Time at proposed-level work | 1 quarter, from the evidence below. Default minimum: 2 |

### The case in three sentences

P-17 led the invoice export rewrite with two other engineers in the second quarter and shipped it on the agreed date. Before and after that quarter, the work was at E2: well-scoped features, owned end to end. The case for E3 rests on one project.

### Evidence by axis

| Axis | E3 expects | Dated evidence | Result | Meets it? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Scope | A system or a project of several months | 2026-04 to 2026-06: invoice export rewrite, three engineers | Shipped 2026-06-12 | Yes, once |
| Ambiguity | Turns a goal into a plan, names risks early | 2026-04-03: wrote the plan from "customers need CSV exports" and listed two risks, one of which happened | Risk handled on time | Yes |
| Delivery | Reports slips early with options | 2026-05-20: told the manager a dependency would slip a week and offered two options | Chose option two, no customer impact | Yes |
| Craft | Designs systems others can extend | Export module has two new formats added by others since June | Two additions without help | Partly. Too early to tell how it holds up |
| Influence | Mentors E1 and E2 engineers, shapes scope with product | One mentoring pairing since August. No example of shaping product scope | None yet | No |
| Engineering with AI | Sets practices for their area | 2026-07: wrote the team's context file for the export module and a review checklist for AI-written changes | Two engineers now use the checklist | Yes |

### Sustained or once?

Q2 2026 is the only quarter with work at E3. Q1 and Q3 were E2 work. That is evidence of potential and not yet of the level.

### Peer input

Four people, one from another team. All four describe the export project as well run. Two say P-17 waits to be asked before helping others. The product partner says P-17 has not yet joined scoping conversations.

### Gaps

- A second quarter of E3 work. Next candidate: the billing reconciliation project starting 2027-01.
- Influence: one example of shaping scope with product, and one more mentoring relationship.

### Decision

Not yet. Gaps were given to P-17 in writing on 2026-10-14. Next look: the 2027-04 cycle.

## The conversation

Say the decision first: "We will not promote you this cycle." Then the strength: "The export project was E3 work, and the context file and review checklist are good AI practice that other engineers now use." Then the gaps with an example each: "The product partner has not seen you in scoping, and the second quarter of E3-level work is still ahead." Then what would change the answer, and when: "Lead the reconciliation project, bring one scoping decision to product, and we look again in April." Do not give a reason you cannot support with an example.
