# Related work

Last checked: 2026-10-03. Star counts and install numbers change. Check the linked pages for current figures.

CTO OS builds on a field that already has good resources. This page says what each one does and where CTO OS is different, so you can pick the right tool for the job.

## Open resources for CTOs

| Resource | What it is | How CTO OS differs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [awesome-cto](https://github.com/kuchin/awesome-cto) | A curated reading list for CTOs and VPs of Engineering, with about 35k stars at the last check. Public domain. Covers hiring, people, architecture, due diligence, finance and more as links. | It points to external reading. CTO OS gives you templates and skills that produce a finished artifact. Use awesome-cto for reading and CTO OS for doing. |
| [cto-advisor](https://agentskills.so/skills/davila7-claude-code-templates-cto-advisor), inside [claude-code-templates](https://github.com/davila7/claude-code-templates) | A single reference skill, MIT licensed, with a tech debt script, a team scaling script, an ADR template, a vendor evaluation outline, DORA targets and a weekly and quarterly cadence. | It is a static reference with fixed targets, such as one manager to eight engineers, for every company. CTO OS adapts to company stage, interviews you, and measures your own baseline before it prescribes. |
| [claude-skills](https://github.com/alirezarezvani/claude-skills) | A large open library of agent skills, with executive persona skills for CEO, CTO, CFO and others. | Broad coverage across roles. CTO OS goes deep on the CTO job and labels each artifact Draft, Used or Proven. |

## How to use them together

- Read awesome-cto when you want the best outside thinking on a topic.
- Use cto-advisor when you want its scripts for a quick tech debt or team scaling estimate.
- Use CTO OS when you need to diagnose your organization, decide, and produce the document the CEO or board will read.

## What CTO OS adds

1. **Diagnose before prescribing.** Skills interview you and read your repositories and documents to build a baseline.
2. **Stage-aware guidance.** Seed, growth and turnaround organizations get different plans.
3. **AI-native engineering.** The [ADOPT assessment](../playbooks/adopt-assessment.md) scores an organization on a five-level curve with evidence.
4. **Evidence labels.** Every artifact states whether it is a Draft, has been Used, or is Proven.
5. **A named author with a track record.** Artifacts come from a practicing CTO and carry field notes about what changed after use.
