# Technology Update: [Company] [Quarter YYYY]

Prepared by: [CTO]. Reviewed by CEO on: [date, at least three days before the board meeting].

## How to use this template

- Keep the same structure every quarter so trends are visible.
- Every metric states its definition, its target and its status.
- Use **Unknown** for anything you cannot measure yet and say when you will.
- Status rule: **Green** at or better than target. **Amber** worse than target with a recovery plan and a date. **Red** worse than target with no recovery plan, or a risk to a customer or financial commitment.
- Explain every Amber and Red and state the planned response.
- Aim for one page of headline and at most five appendix pages.

## Definition of done

- All metrics have a definition, a target and a status, or are marked Unknown with a date.
- Every Amber and Red has a response.
- The CEO reviewed the update at least three days before the meeting.
- The asks are specific and fit on one page.

## How you will know it worked

Leading: the CEO makes few edits and no surprise corrections. Lagging: board questions move from "what happened" to trade-offs, and asks are granted or declined at the meeting.
