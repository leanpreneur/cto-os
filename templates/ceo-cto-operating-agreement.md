## 5. Communication rules

- **No surprises.** Nothing appears in a staff meeting or a board meeting that the other has not heard first.
- **Bad news timing.** Within [24] hours of becoming known. Customer-affecting incidents: immediately.
- **Format.** Conclusion first, then effect on customers, revenue or date, then the plan, then the ask.
- **Channels.** Urgent: [phone or chat]. Routine: [email or document]. Decisions: written in the decision record.
- **Written follow-up** within one business day of a decision made in conversation.

## 6. How we disagree

1. Raise concerns before the decision, with evidence and a recommendation.
2. The decision owner in Section 3 decides.
3. After the decision, both commit and neither undermines it in front of the team.
4. A decision reopens only on new evidence, stated as such.
5. If we cannot resolve it in two conversations, we involve: [named person, such as a board member or advisor].

## 8. Feedback and review

- Check-ins at 30, 60 and 90 days, then quarterly.
- Each quarter, each of us answers: What should I do more of? Less of? What do you need from me that you are not getting?
- Once a year, a 360 review that includes the executive team and engineering leaders.

## 9. Success measures for the partnership

- The CEO has no unfamiliar item in staff or board meetings.
- The weekly one-on-one happens, and is not cancelled twice in a row.
- The three outcomes in Section 1 are on track, or off track with a plan.
- Decisions are recorded.

## 10. Reset triggers

We hold a reset conversation within one week if any of these occur:

- An outcome in Section 1 is red with no recovery plan.
- Either of us learns of a significant issue from a third party first.
- A decision in Section 3 is made outside its owner twice in a quarter.
- Either of us believes the scope in Section 2 no longer fits the company.

## How you will know it worked

Leading: the weekly one-on-one occurs and decisions are recorded. Lagging: fewer escalations and no repeat of a decision made outside its owner.
